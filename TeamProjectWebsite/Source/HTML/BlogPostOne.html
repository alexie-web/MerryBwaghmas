What Frutiger Aero Says about us then and now

Do you remember StumbleUpon? I have too many nostalgic memories of browsing it with my sister. That GIF of a chair that folded into a briefcase blew our minds. So very unfortunately, StumbleUpon started to lose popularity in 2013, and many companies caught on that it could be used as a marketing tool, filling it with bloat and clickbait. Ultimately, it shut down in 2018 and is currently without any popular successors. As much as I loved that chair/suitcase folding gif, there were also many other flashier, sparklier GIFs I enjoyed made by users on Blingee. What do you know, Blingee has shut down in June of 2024. Angelfire, an older web hosting site, was closed down in April 2026, rendering all their websites, some of which I enjoyed, inaccessible. Many other countless websites have become broken, unusable, and undocumented. Adobe Flash content was completely blocked in January 2021. Many social MMO games from the 2000's aimed at kids are closed down, such as Toontown, Club Penguin, FreeRealms, Fusionfall, and World of Jumpstart. Let's face it, the internet as we know it is dead and gone. The world is falling apart. And the planet is dying, partially due to a lack of interest in sustainable ideologies.


Or is it? Take a look at StumbleUpon's logo (top left in the collage). There you can see Frutiger Aero, an art style from the early to late 2000's, that represents the young and free internet and an idealistic future of pristine waters and healthy plants. And this Frutiger Aero art style, it's beginning to make a comeback.
